Apprenticeship Schedule Overview

Week 1: Onboarding and Foundation

This week is all about getting you settled and understanding the journey ahead.

  • Onboarding Call: A comprehensive session to kick things off.
  • Outlook of the Next 8 Weeks: A clear roadmap of your apprenticeship.
  • Mentorship Breakdown: Learn from Robbie how mentorship works.
  • GRIT System Breakdown: Gichogu will explain our unique GRIT system.
  • Communication Channels & Feedback: Joy will guide you on how we communicate.
  • Track-Specific Introductions:
    • Okemwa - Backend
    • Collo - Frontend
    • Athaliah - UI/UX
  • AMA (Ask Me Anything) Session: Get your questions answered!

Weeks 2-5: Agile Sprints (Individual Work)

These weeks focus on building your individual skills through a structured sprint cycle.

  • Kick Off: Each week starts with a clear objective.
  • Task Cycle (Monday to Wednesday):
    • Task 1: Assigned by your mentors (Okemwa for Backend, Collo for Frontend, Athaliah for UI/UX).
    • Communication: All task details and submissions happen on Slack.
    • Anatomy of a Task:
      • Task Name: E.g., Stage Ten Task
      • Task Description: E.g., Create an app that shows your portfolio.
      • Acceptance Criteria: E.g., The app should have an authentication page, two pages, and links to your contacts.
      • Submission Mode: Google Forms link.
      • Submission Deadline: E.g., Wednesday, July 16th, 2025.
    • Mentor Action: On Wednesday's deadline, mentors close the form, review Task 1, and drop Task 2 on Slack the next morning.
  • Task Cycle (Thursday to Saturday):
    • Task 2: Assigned by your mentors.
    • Mentor Action: Mentors review Task 1 at their own time. On Saturday's deadline, mentors close the form and review Task 2.
  • Sunday Review: Mentors schedule a call with cohort builders on their track to review Task 1 and 2. We'll also have a cohort-wide call on GRIT one.
  • Feedback: Builders provide feedback on Task 1 and 2.

NB: This cycle repeats up to Stage 5, ensuring continuous learning and feedback.

Week 4: Shark Tank Week! 🦈

A special week to ignite your entrepreneurial spirit!

  • Human Design Thinking: On top of your weekly tasks, you'll apply human design thinking principles.
  • SaaS Solution Ideas: Come up with innovative SaaS solutions to real societal problems.
  • Sunday Presentation: During the GRIT call, builders will present their ideas to a panel of judges (builders and mentors).

Week 5: Collaborative Week & Team Setup

Transition from individual work to powerful team collaboration.

  • Mentor Task: Mentors will identify 5 talented builders to become team leads.
  • Team Creation: 5 team channels will be created, and builders assigned to each team.
  • Team Structure: Each team resembles a startup, with a backend, frontend, digital marketer, and UI/UX specialist.

Weeks 6-7: Team Project Development

These weeks are dedicated to building your collaborative application.

  • Tasks 6-9: These core project tasks will be completed during these weeks as a team.

Week 8: Hack Week! 🎉

The grand finale of your building journey!

  • Ghost Week for Mentors: Mentees will be on their own, truly owning their product development and polishing.
  • Hackathon: On Sunday, teams will present their fully functional products.
  • Awards: An award ceremony at the end for the best team!

Post-Program Meet-up

Celebrate your achievements and network after the program concludes.

  • We'll have a post-program meet-up on the first weekend of September after the program ends.
