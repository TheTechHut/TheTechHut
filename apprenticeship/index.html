The programme

The eight weeks

Two phases and ten stages: four weeks on the foundations of your own track, then four weeks building a cross-functional project as a team.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1–4

Foundational Building

  1. Stages 1–2
    The LaunchpadOnboarding, environment setup, core fundamentals specific to your chosen track.
  2. Stage 3
    Core ConceptsThe essential theory and practice of your track, in depth.
  3. Stage 4
    Specialized SkillsMastering key tools and techniques relevant to your field.
  4. Stage 5
    Individual ShowcaseBuilding a personal project or portfolio piece to prove your skills.
Phase 2 · Weeks 5–8

Collaborative Project

  1. Stages 6–7
    Project Kick-off & DesignTeam formation, requirements analysis, initial implementation for a cross-functional project.
  2. Stage 8
    Collaborative SprintFeature development, content creation, design iteration, and continuous testing within your team.
  3. Stage 9
    Refinement & TestingQuality assurance, bug fixing/optimization, and final content/design review.
  4. Stage 10
    The Grand FinaleProject presentation and graduation.
