Your 8-Week Journey

Our apprenticeship is divided into two distinct phases and ten progressive stages, ensuring a holistic learning experience.

Phase 1: Foundational Building (Weeks 1-4)

  • ●

    **Stage 1-2: The Launchpad** - Onboarding, environment setup, core programming fundamentals.

  • ●

    **Stage 3: Logic & Structure** - Mastering functions and modular programming.

  • ●

    **Stage 4: Data Mastery** - Exploring data structures and basic algorithms.

  • ●

    **Stage 5: Individual Showcase** - Building a personal project to prove your skills.

Phase 2: Collaborative Project (Weeks 5-8)

  • ●

    **Stage 6-7: Project Kick-off & Design** - Team formation, requirements analysis, initial implementation.

  • ●

    **Stage 8: Collaborative Sprint** - Feature development, integration, and continuous testing.

  • ●

    **Stage 9: Refinement & Testing** - Quality assurance, bug fixing, and optimization.

  • ●

    **Stage 10: The Grand Finale** - Project presentation and graduation.
