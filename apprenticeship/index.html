Your 8-Week Journey

Our apprenticeship is divided into two distinct phases and ten progressive stages, ensuring a holistic learning experience.

Phase 1: Foundational Building (Weeks 1-4)

  • ●

    **Stage 1-2: The Launchpad** - Onboarding, environment setup, core fundamentals specific to your chosen track.

  • ●

    **Stage 3: Core Concepts** - Deep dive into essential theories and practices of your track.

  • ●

    **Stage 4: Specialized Skills** - Mastering key tools and techniques relevant to your field.

  • ●

    **Stage 5: Individual Showcase** - Building a personal project or portfolio piece to prove your skills.

Phase 2: Collaborative Project (Weeks 5-8)

  • ●

    **Stage 6-7: Project Kick-off & Design** - Team formation, requirements analysis, initial implementation for a cross-functional project.

  • ●

    **Stage 8: Collaborative Sprint** - Feature development, content creation, design iteration, and continuous testing within your team.

  • ●

    **Stage 9: Refinement & Testing** - Quality assurance, bug fixing/optimization, and final content/design review.

  • ●

    **Stage 10: The Grand Finale** - Project presentation and graduation.
