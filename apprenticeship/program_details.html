Two Phases of Transformation

Phase 1: Foundational Building (Weeks 1-4)
The first half of your journey focuses on solidifying core coding concepts through individual tasks. We equip you with robust programming fundamentals to ensure you're ready for any challenge.

Phase 2: Collaborative Building (Weeks 5-8)
In the second half, you'll apply your skills in a team environment. You'll work together on a real-world company project, developing vital collaborative skills and creating a portfolio-ready piece.
