49 games · free · nothing to install

Games that play in your browser.

No Steam, no Epic, no downloads. Shooters, strategy, racing, puzzles and a few famous classics, including Doom and Diablo. Games marked Plays here open on this site. The rest open on the maker’s own site, which is where they asked to be played.

Every game here is free and legal: official, open source, or a free shareware release. Why some famous ones are missing

Why isn’t Call of Duty here?

You will find browser copies of Call of Duty, GTA, Halo, Counter-Strike and Need for Speed around the web. They are unofficial uploads of games that are still sold, made without the publishers’ permission, so we don’t list them. The same goes for sites that ask you to load files from a game you own.

What is here is free to play because the people who own it made it free: official web games, open-source projects, and shareware such as the first episode of Doom and the Diablo demo, which id Software and Blizzard gave away in the 1990s.
