Most “remote” jobs won’t hire you.

Remote, France. Remote, United States. Italy (Remote). Remote India. Every big remote job board is full of roles that are only remote if you already have the right to work in one particular country — and nobody tells you which is which until you have spent an evening on the application.

So we scanned every live opening at the employers we track and sorted them by one question: will this company employ someone sitting in Nairobi?

Why this is not another remote job board

The 2,445 to 300 problem

Here is the thing that costs Kenyan developers the most time, and it is not a lack of remote jobs. It is that "remote" has two completely different meanings and the job boards use the same word for both.

How the posting reads What it actually means Can you take it from Nairobi?
Home based — EMEA Europe, Middle East and Africa. Kenya is in Africa. Yes
Remote, Global Anywhere with an internet connection and an overlapping timezone. Yes
Remote in Africa Written for exactly your situation. Yes
Remote, France You must already have the right to work in France. No
Italy (Remote) Italian payroll. Italian tax number. Italian residence. No
Remote — EMEA; Remote — France; Remote — Germany EMEA is on the list, so this one is open. Read every option, not the first. Yes
Nairobi, Kenya A Kenyan job on a Kenyan salary. Good — but not what you came here for. Local
The finding that surprised us. We also checked the two aggregators most people are told to use. On the day we built this, one returned 4 Kenya-eligible roles out of 100, and the other returned none at all out of 99. The roles that will actually hire you are almost never on the famous remote job boards — they are on the companies' own hiring systems, which is where we go and get them.
