# Outreach drafts — week 2

**Status: drafts. Nothing here has been sent.** Fill in the names and addresses, read each
one over, then send from `info@thetechhut.co` yourself.

Rate card: <https://thetechhut.co/advertise/>

A few rules that make these work:

- Send on a **Tuesday or Wednesday morning**. Monday gets deleted, Friday gets forgotten.
- One email, one ask. Do not attach the rate card — link it. Attachments trip spam filters
  and nobody opens a PDF from a stranger.
- Find a **named human** first. "Hi there" halves your reply rate. For employers look for the
  Talent Acquisition or People lead on LinkedIn; for bootcamps look for Partnerships or Growth.
- If there is no reply in five working days, send the follow-up at the bottom. Once. Then stop.
- Track it: keep a sheet with company, person, date sent, date followed up, outcome.
