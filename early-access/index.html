Before you ask

Fair questions

Is this a job guarantee?

No, and anyone selling you one in Kenya is lying. This buys you earlier sight of real openings and a sharper CV. The applying is still yours to do.

Why not just read the free channel?

You should — it is free and it is good. This exists for people in an active search, where a day's head start and a filtered list are worth 250 bob a month. If you are not searching right now, do not pay for it.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa. Send the form, we send you the number and the exact amount, you pay, you are in. No card, no standing order, no automatic renewal.

How do I leave?

Tell us, or simply do not pay the next month. Nothing renews by itself, so leaving requires no effort at all.

Do you sell my details to recruiters?

No. Employers pay us to reach the audience, never to receive it. Your number and email stay with us — see the privacy policy.

I am a student with no money. Anything for me?

The free channel, the free CV pack and the free jobs board, all of which are genuinely free and stay that way. Come back when you are earning.
