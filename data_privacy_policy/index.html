Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal information The Tech Hut collects across everything we run, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it and how you can see, correct or delete it. It covers thetechhut.co and its pages, our email lists, the things we sell, the prompt marketplace, the games arcade, our events, and the forms and WhatsApp conversations that come from them.

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

The short version

  • You can read the site, use the jobs board and browse hackathons without giving us anything about you.
  • We collect personal details only when you sign up, buy, post, join or write to us, and only what that needs.
  • We never sell your data, and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • A small number of service providers handle data for us (email, payments, hosting). They act on our instructions.
  • You can unsubscribe in one click and ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time: info@thetechhut.co.

Who we are

The Tech Hut Community ("The Tech Hut", "we") runs thetechhut.co and is responsible for the personal data described here. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Contact us about anything in this policy at info@thetechhut.co or +254 115 017 058.

When you browse the site

Server records. Our pages are hosted on Google's Firebase Hosting and our server functions run on Cloudflare. Like any web host, they record technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser type and the page asked for, to deliver pages, keep the service secure and stop abuse.

Analytics. The home page and an events confirmation page use Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors scroll and click (heatmaps and session replays, with typed text masked) so we can see what is confusing. Clarity sets cookies for this. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not run advertising or retargeting trackers anywhere on the site.

Things your browser loads from others. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, photos from Unsplash and, in some articles and marketplace listings, videos from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode. A marketplace listing with a video shows its preview picture from YouTube, and the video itself loads only when you press play. Those services receive your IP address when your browser fetches them, under their own privacy policies.

Your browser's own storage. A few features remember things on your device rather than on our servers: your prompt marketplace or events sign-in, your arcade nickname and player id, and a tab id for the live games counter. They stay on your device; clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Jobs board, job pages and hackathons

The jobs board and hackathons list show openings we read from employers' and organisers' public pages. You do not need an account, and we do not collect anything about you to show them. When you apply, you do it on the employer's or organiser's own site, under their privacy policy; we never see your application.

Email lists

We run three separate lists: the CV Pack list with the Monday openings digest (thetechhut.co/cv-pack), the Daily Savannah weekly roundup (thetechhut.co/blog) and the Daily job alert (thetechhut.co/jobs/daily). For each we collect your email address and, if you choose to give them, your first name and the kind of role you are after.

We first email you a confirmation link and only add you after you click it; that click is your consent, and we record it. Joining one list does not add you to another. We use the details only to send that list's emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link that removes you in one click. The lists are held by Brevo, our email provider, and signups pass through our Cloudflare Worker.

Buying something from us

When you buy the Remote-From-Kenya List, ATS Pass, the Job Hunt Bundle, Early Access, the CV Blueprint or the African Tech Community Database, we ask for your email address, your WhatsApp number and, optionally, your name. We use them to take the payment, send your receipt, deliver what you bought and contact you about it.

  • Payments are made through Paystack, by M-PESA or card. Your card or M-PESA details go to Paystack and never reach us; Paystack also receives your email, number and name for the transaction.
  • Receipts are emailed through Resend or Brevo.
  • Early Access is a WhatsApp group. Other members can see the phone number you join with, as in any WhatsApp group.
  • Fraud and bot protection. To stop fake orders, checkout may use Cloudflare Turnstile (a bot check) and checks that your email's domain can receive mail. We count checkout attempts per IP address and per email and number to limit abuse; emails and numbers are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, and all these counts expire within a day. For each order we keep the product, status and times, with no name, email or number, for about 13 months so that each receipt is sent only once.

We keep payment records as long as Kenyan tax and accounting rules require.

Prompt marketplace

On thetechhut.co/prompts you sign in with your email address: we email you a one-time link through Brevo, and your browser keeps a signed sign-in token. We store your email, the name you choose to show, the prompts you post, and what you buy and when. Other members see only your chosen name, never your email.

  • Emails we send you: sign-in links, a welcome email when you join, an email with your prompt's link when you post a new one, and receipts. When you buy, we email you the prompt and your receipt. When you sell, we email you that a sale happened, with the amount, but never who bought it. Now and then we also email members about new marketplace features; every one of those has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing does not stop your sign-in links or receipts.
  • Payments and payouts go through Paystack. If you sell paid prompts, you give Paystack your bank and account number so it can pay you directly; we keep only the bank name, the last four digits and Paystack's reference for your payout account. Buyers' card and M-PESA details never reach us.
  • Example images and videos. Sellers can add up to three example images and a YouTube link to a prompt, which everyone can see. Your browser shrinks each image before uploading it, which also removes hidden details such as the location a phone photo was taken. We store the images with Cloudflare and delete them when you remove them from the prompt, or remove an unsold prompt.
  • Moderation. Anyone can report a prompt; we count reports without recording who made them, apart from a per-IP-address limit that expires within a day. Our administrators can see members' emails, prompts and sales to run the marketplace, resend emails and deal with reports.

To close your account and erase your details, write to info@thetechhut.co. Prompts other members have already bought stay available to them.

Games arcade

On thetechhut.co/games we count how many people are on the page and how long each game is played, using a random id that your browser keeps for one tab. That count is anonymous.

The weekly leaderboards include you only if you join them. With a nickname, we store that nickname, a random player id kept in your browser, and how long you played each game that week, your longest sitting and which days you played. If you sign in with Google instead, Google gives us a fixed account number, which we scramble before storing; we never receive or keep your email address, and we keep the name Google sends only if you use it as your nickname. Nicknames and play time are shown publicly on the leaderboard. Weekly records are kept for 12 months. Choose "Leave the leaderboard" on your arcade card to remove yourself at any time.

Events

Most of our events are listed and registered through Luma (luma.com), so your registration is handled under Luma's privacy policy, and we receive the details you give there, usually your name, email address and any answers to the registration questions. We use them to run the event: confirmations, reminders, entry, follow-up and, where we say so on the event page, sharing with the event's partners. After an event we may tell you about future events; you can opt out at any time. If we take photos or video at an event, we say so at the event, and you can ask us not to include you.

Forms, applications and messages

  • Advertise, a jobs board for your community, and Early Access interest forms send what you type (such as your name, organisation, email, phone or WhatsApp number and your message) to our inbox through Formspree. We use it only to reply to you and arrange what you asked about.
  • Apprenticeship applications are made on Google Forms. We use your answers to assess your application, contact you about it and run the programme.
  • Emails and WhatsApp messages you send us are kept so we can reply and follow up. Our WhatsApp channel is one-way: we cannot see who follows it.

Why we use your data

We use personal data only for the purposes described above, on these grounds under the Data Protection Act:

  • Your consent, for email lists, marketing about future events and optional features such as joining the leaderboard. You can withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect what happened before.
  • To provide what you asked for, such as delivering a purchase, running your marketplace account or processing an application.
  • Our legitimate interests, in keeping the site secure, stopping fraud and abuse, and understanding how the site is used so we can improve it.
  • Legal obligations, such as keeping payment records.

Who else handles your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing. These providers handle it for us, under their own security and privacy terms, only to provide their service:

  • Brevo: email lists and emails we send.
  • Resend: purchase receipts, when used.
  • Paystack: payments and marketplace payouts.
  • Cloudflare: our server functions, data storage for the marketplace, events and games, and bot checks.
  • Google: Firebase Hosting, Google Forms for applications, Google Fonts, and Google sign-in on the arcade if you choose it.
  • Microsoft Clarity: site analytics on the pages named above.
  • Formspree: delivering contact and interest forms to our inbox.
  • Luma: event listings and registration.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): our channel, the Early Access group and conversations with us.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our members and others.

Data stored outside Kenya

Several of these providers store or process data outside Kenya, for example in the European Union, the United States or South Africa. We use them only for the purposes above, choose established providers that protect data with appropriate safeguards, and keep what we send them to what each service needs.

How long we keep it

  • Email lists: until you unsubscribe or ask us to erase you.
  • Purchases: as long as tax and accounting rules require; anti-fraud counts for up to a day; order status records for about 13 months.
  • Prompt marketplace account: until you ask us to close it; sold prompts stay with their buyers. Unused sign-in links expire after 20 minutes; sign-in tokens after 60 days.
  • Games leaderboard: 12 months of weekly records, or until you leave the leaderboard.
  • Event registrations, form submissions and applications: as long as needed for that event, enquiry or programme intake, and then deleted or kept only in summary, unless you have agreed to hear from us again.
  • Server records: as long as our hosting providers keep them for security and operations.

Keeping it safe

We keep secret keys out of our code and pages, use encrypted connections throughout, store only what each feature needs (for example, scrambled ids instead of emails where we can), and limit administrator access to the people who run each service. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach affects your data and puts you at risk, we will tell you and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used (this policy);
  • get a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it deleted, or object to or restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable form, where that applies;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

To have your data deleted, follow the steps on thetechhut.co/data-deletion. To use any of the other rights, email info@thetechhut.co from the address you used with us, or tell us how to confirm it is you. We will respond within the time the Act allows. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Children

Our paid products and the prompt marketplace are for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before giving us any personal information. If we learn we hold a child's data without that consent, we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When we add a service or change how we handle data, we update this page and the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you by email or on the site before it takes effect.
