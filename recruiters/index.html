Our model

How the partnership works: a talent pipeline built backwards from the job

Your blueprint: the curriculum

We partner with you to dissect your key job descriptions (for example, sales leads or operations managers). We then reverse-engineer these requirements into a rigorous, step-by-step curriculum.

The build: the apprenticeship

Our cohort of “builders” completes the intensive apprenticeship, guided by your industry standards and focused on real-world, task-based projects.

Placement: the talent pipeline

You gain exclusive access to a pool of vetted candidates who were trained to fit your organisational structure and role specifications.

Example: our collaboration with VisionDrill ensures graduates can immediately contribute to their sales and operations teams.
