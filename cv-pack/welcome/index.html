While you wait: the six rules

1. One column. Always. Two-column CVs are read straight across the page by most parsers.

2. No tables, text boxes or icons. Write the word Phone, not a phone icon.

3. Use the boring headings. Work Experience, Education, Skills, Certifications.

4. Dates as MM/YYYY, right of the role. Date parsing is how years of experience are worked out.

5. Mirror the posting's exact nouns. If the ad says Node.js, write Node.js.

6. Submit a PDF, unless asked for Word. Name it Firstname-Lastname-Role.pdf.

The full guide, with the seven systems and a copy-paste skeleton, is at thetechhut.co/cv-pack.
