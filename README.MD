# THE TECH HUT

THE TECH HUT is an Ed Tech and Talent sourcing company that specializes in training talent for organizations in need of staffing. We partner with Apex tech, a leading software solutions provider and IT consultancy, to deliver top-notch training programs and talent sourcing services.

## About Apex tech

Apex tech is a renowned company that builds software solutions for large organizations. They also offer consultancy services in IT, UI/UX Design, and Saas solutions. As a partner of THE TECH HUT, Apex tech plays a crucial role in providing opportunities for trainees to be recruited within their organization or outsourced to other companies.

## Empowering Entrepreneurs

At THE TECH HUT, we believe in empowering entrepreneurs to build innovative solutions that have a positive impact on society. If you're an entrepreneur looking to join us, we welcome you to collaborate with us in creating cutting-edge solutions that empower the community.

## Getting Started

To get started with THE TECH HUT, follow these steps:

1. Clone the repository: `git clone https://github.com/techhut/web.git`
2. Install the necessary dependencies: `npm install`
3. Start the development server: `npm start`
