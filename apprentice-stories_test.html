APPRENTICE STORIES · LEARN BY BUILDING

Learning by building — and growing together.

For these Tech Hut learners, the journey has meant more than picking up technical skills. It has meant finding mentors, collaborating with peers and getting the confidence to build real things.

▶

A community that makes learning practical

Kelvin Kemboi introduces himself as a mathematics and computer science student. Valerie shares that her technology journey began in 2021 and grew through meeting other people who are just as curious about tech.

Across the interviews, apprentices describe a supportive environment built around teamwork, mentorship and practical experience. One learner speaks about spending around six months with The Tech Hut, working alongside colleagues and learning from mentors while contributing to projects.

Another describes being introduced to the community while searching for mentorship. The opportunity to collaborate and develop a real-world application helped turn learning into something tangible.

The journey has not been without challenges. One apprentice says moving between frontend and backend tracks was demanding, and balancing the programme with other commitments required better time management. But the support of the community helped make that challenge manageable.

“A place to be mentored — a place to get support.”

The message that comes through is simple: growth happens faster when you have people to learn with, people to learn from, and opportunities to put your skills into practice.

What stood out

  • Peer collaboration and teamwork
  • Guidance from mentors
  • Hands-on work on real-world projects
  • Exploring frontend and backend development
  • Learning to balance growth with other commitments

Names and details are included only where they were clear in the supplied transcript.
