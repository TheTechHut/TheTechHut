Delete your data

You can ask The Tech Hut to delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. Some things you can remove yourself in one click; for everything else, send us one email and we will do it for you.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Delete everything: one email

Email info@thetechhut.co with the subject "Delete my data", from the email address you used with us. Tell us which services you used if you know (for example the prompt marketplace, an email list or a purchase); if you are not sure, we will check them all.

  • We confirm we have your request within 3 working days, and complete it within 30 days, usually much sooner.
  • If you write from a different address, we will ask you to confirm it is you before we delete anything.
  • We email you when it is done, listing what was deleted.

Things you can remove yourself

  • Email lists (CV Pack, Daily Savannah roundup, daily job alert): press "unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email. It removes you from that list at once; email us to erase your details entirely.
  • Games arcade leaderboard: on thetechhut.co/games, open your player card and choose "Leave the leaderboard". Your nickname and every week's play record are deleted straight away.
  • Sign in with Google (games arcade): we never receive your email, and we store only a scrambled account number. To remove that too, leave the leaderboard as above, then remove The Tech Hut's access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party connections.
  • Data in your browser (sign-ins, nickname, player id): clear this site's data in your browser's settings.

What we delete

  • Prompt marketplace: your account, email address, chosen name, saved payout details (bank name and last four digits) and the prompts you posted. Prompts other members have already bought stay available to those buyers, without your email.
  • Email lists: your email address, name and preferences, from every list.
  • Purchases: your contact details in our records and inbox.
  • Forms, applications and messages: what you sent through our forms, your Apprenticeship application and our email and WhatsApp conversations with you.
  • Events: your registration details in our copies; registrations made on Luma can also be deleted from your Luma account.

What we may have to keep

  • Payment records that Kenyan tax and accounting law requires us to keep, for as long as it requires. Paystack keeps its own transaction records under its own legal duties.
  • Anonymous records that no longer identify you, such as order status entries without names or contact details and overall visitor counts.
  • Security logs kept briefly by our hosting providers.

Read more in our privacy policy. If you are unhappy with how we handle your request, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).
