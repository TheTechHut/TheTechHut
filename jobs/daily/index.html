How the daily alert works

A simpler way to hear about jobs the day they open.

Most roles at tech and impact employers never reach a job board. They go up on the company's own hiring page, and the early applicants get read first.

  1. 01

    Tell us where to send it

    Your email, your first name and, if you like, the kind of role you want. One click in the confirmation email and you are on.

  2. 02

    We read the employers' own pages

    Every morning we check 48 employers' hiring systems, compare them with the past week and keep only the roles that are actually new. Re-posts don't count.

  3. 03

    Apply first

    By about 7:00 AM Nairobi time the new roles are in your inbox, grouped by field, each linking straight to the employer's application page.
