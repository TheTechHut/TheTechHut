GTA 6 Is Out. Here Is What It Costs in Kenya, and What It Says About Game Jobs

The biggest launch in entertainment history needs a console most Kenyans do not own. What GTA 6 is, what it costs here, the traps to avoid, and the careers hidden in its 78-minute credits.

The Tech Hut · Sunday, 22 November 2026

Grand Theft Auto VI came out on Thursday, 19 November 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays and more than a decade of work. By the end of its first day, Take-Two Interactive, which owns Rockstar Games, estimated it had made US$3.79 billion in retail sales, which the company called the biggest launch in entertainment history. It is also, for many Kenyan players, an expensive and slightly complicated game to buy.

This article explains what GTA 6 is, what it costs to play in Kenya, the traps to avoid, and what the thousands of people who built it tell us about jobs in the games industry.

What GTA 6 is

GTA 6 is an open-world crime game set in Leonida, a fictional American state based on Florida, with Vice City as its Miami. For the first time in a main GTA game, there are two playable leads from the start: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple drawn into crime together. Lucia is the series' first female protagonist you are required to play.

The road to launch was long. Rockstar began early development in 2018, after Red Dead Redemption 2, and showed the first trailer in December 2023 with a 2025 release window. In May 2025 it moved the date to 26 May 2026, and in November 2025 it moved it again, to 19 November 2026, to give the game more polish. The wait built huge demand: Rockstar's second trailer drew a record 475 million views across platforms in its first 24 hours.

The gap between the critics' 99 and the players' 5.6 is worth a note. Critics have praised the game almost without exception. Player scores in the first days of a huge launch are often pulled down by complaints that have little to do with the game itself, such as price, the lack of a PC version and the 30 frames per second cap on both consoles, which Rockstar confirmed in August. Read a few reviews of each kind before you spend.

What it costs to play GTA 6 in Kenya

There are two costs: the game and the console. GTA 6 runs only on a PlayStation 5 or an Xbox Series X|S. There is no version for PC, older consoles or phones.

The game

Nairobi game shops have sold GTA 6 as a download code or as store credit for a PlayStation or Xbox account. Listed prices we found ranged from about KSh 10,000 to KSh 17,000 before 16% VAT, depending on the shop, the edition and when the listing was made. In October, Tech With Muchiri found Standard Edition prices of KSh 9,999 at iTey Store and KSh 11,999 at Level Up Techstore, both before VAT. Prices change, so confirm the final amount, including VAT, before you pay.

Even the boxed copy has no disc. Rockstar's physical edition contains a download code, so you need an internet connection and enough storage to download the game either way.

The console

If you do not already own one, the console is the bigger bill. In October, before VAT, Nairobi retailers listed an Xbox Series S at about KSh 56,000 to KSh 58,000, an Xbox Series X at about KSh 80,000, and a PS5 Slim at about KSh 95,000 to KSh 108,000, according to Tech With Muchiri. That puts the cheapest way in, an Xbox Series S plus the game, at well over KSh 70,000 once VAT is added.

The account region

Sony does not run an official PlayStation Store for Kenya, so most Kenyan players use an account registered to another country, such as the UK, the US, the UAE or South Africa. Codes and store credit only work on an account from the same region, and a code redeemed on the wrong account is usually not refundable. Before you buy, check which country your account is set to and tell the shop.

Scams to avoid

  • Any “GTA 6 for PC” or “GTA 6 APK” download is fake. There is no PC or mobile version. These files are a common way to spread malware and steal accounts.
  • Codes that are far cheaper than everywhere else. Cheap codes are often stolen or already used. Buy from a shop with a physical address and a receipt.
  • Anyone asking for your PlayStation or Xbox password to “install” the game for you. Redeem codes yourself.
  • Money-making offers inside the game. Rockstar says the game has no microtransactions at launch, so offers to sell you in-game cash are not from Rockstar.

GTA 6 is rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB, the US ratings body, and it is full of violence and adult themes. If you are buying it for a teenager at home, that rating is worth reading.

How big is GTA in Kenya?

Smaller than the hype online suggests. An Action Network analysis of Google searches over the past year, reported by Techweez, ranked Kenya 113th of 150 countries for GTA 6 interest, with about 49 searches per 100,000 people, or roughly 28,000 a month. Algeria led Africa at 314 per 100,000. Searches are not sales, but the ranking matches what the prices above suggest: for most Kenyans, a current-generation console is a big purchase, and many players will reach GTA 6 later, through a friend's console, a gaming lounge or a cheaper second-hand PS5.

The jobs behind GTA 6

The part of GTA 6 most relevant to a tech career is not the game but the team. By Inven Global's count, the end credits run for 78 minutes and name 10,573 people. Rockstar's own development team alone numbers 2,739, rising to 4,651 with quality assurance, platform services, marketing and business staff.

Programmers are not the biggest group. Inven Global counted 957 people in quality assurance (QA), 929 in art, 580 in animation and 523 in technology and programming. More than 3,000 actors, voice performers and face models are credited too. A modern game is built by artists, testers, animators, producers, writers and data and operations people as much as by coders.

Much of that work happens far from the studio's headquarters. In October 2025, Techloy reported that Rockstar India had more developers on the game than any other Rockstar studio, about 1,600, and Inven Global found 272 credited names from Technicolor Games, an outside studio, most of them Indian. Big studios send art, animation and testing work to countries with skilled, lower-cost teams. That is how India became part of the world's biggest game, and it is the opening African studios and freelancers are competing for.

If you want to work in games from Kenya, some roles are easier to start in than others:

  • Quality assurance tester: the largest department on GTA 6. Testers play builds, find bugs and write clear reports. Careful writing and patience matter more than a degree.
  • 3D artist or animator: learn Blender, which is free, build a portfolio of finished models or animations, and look for outsourcing studios and remote contracts.
  • Game programmer: start with a free engine such as Godot or Unity, finish small games, and put them online. Game jams, the game-making version of a hackathon, are the fastest way to build a portfolio and meet teams.
  • Community, support and localisation: large games need moderators, player-support agents and translators, often remote. Our guide to remote customer service jobs covers how those roles hire.

A GTA 6 credit is out of reach for most people. A finished small game, a bug report a studio can act on, or a portfolio of animations is not.

Should you buy it now?

If you already own a PS5 or an Xbox Series X|S and enjoy open-world games, the reviews say GTA 6 is worth it, and the download code route works once your account region is sorted out. If you would need to buy a console first, there is no rush. The game will not go anywhere, prices for consoles and codes may fall, and a PC version, which Rockstar has not announced, has followed every recent GTA on console. Whatever you decide, buy only from a shop you can visit, and ignore every “free download” you see.
