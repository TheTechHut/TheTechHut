Hackers hid card-stealing code on the BBC, CNN, Zoom and 175 other websites, and it tried to break into older iPhones

On Monday, visitors to about 178 organisations' websites, including the BBC, CNN, Zoom, IKEA and HSBC, received malicious code that none of those sites wrote. It came through Optimizely, a US tool many companies use to test versions of their pages, after an attacker exploited a flaw in its product. The code ran for about 17 hours. It swapped copied cryptocurrency wallet addresses for the attacker's own, collected card numbers, expiry dates and CVVs typed into payment forms, and on older iPhones and iPads loaded exploit kits that tried to take over the device without the user clicking anything. Thirty-two of the affected sites belong to banks or finance companies; no Kenyan site was on the list found by the British security firm Report URI, which mapped the attack. Optimizely says it has fixed the flaw, removed the code and revoked access tokens. Nobody has said how many phones were compromised. What to do: update your iPhone to the newest version for your model under Settings, then General, then Software Update; on an iPhone too old to update, turn on Lockdown Mode under Privacy & Security. If you paid by card on a large international site on Monday, check your statement, and if you sent crypto that day, compare the address you paid with the one you meant to.

Safaricom's investment customers reach 3.4 million as Ziidi grows inside M-PESA

Active wealth management customers rose from 1.3 million a year earlier, and money under management grew 70% to KSh25.6 billion in the six months to September. Ziidi includes a money market fund, a Sharia-compliant fund and Ziidi Trader, which lets you buy NSE shares from one share in the M-PESA app. Safaricom has not said how many people actually trade shares, so the 3.4 million is not a count of share traders.

Nairobi has the best indoor 4G signal of the African cities Ookla measured

Across more than 100 cities in 60 countries, Nairobi's median indoor signal was about -89 dBm, ahead of Lagos, Cairo and Johannesburg and close to Dubai, Bangkok and Seoul. In London, indoor 4G was unreliable in about a third of buildings measured. Ookla found weak signal can cut download speeds by 65%, but the study does not say which Kenyan network does best.

Amazon and Google will together own nearly 30% of Anthropic after it lists

A filing shows Amazon will hold about 14.7% and Google about 13.7%, both in non-voting shares, with Amazon able to rise to about 23.8% by converting more notes. AMD, Broadcom, Microsoft, Micron, Nvidia and TSMC are also buying about US$26 billion of non-voting stock. Trading on Nasdaq is expected to start tomorrow; Kenyans cannot join the IPO but can buy afterwards through a broker or app that offers US shares.

GAMING

GTA VI unlocks overnight, but there is no official Kenyan price

The game launches on 19 November on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight in the country your account is registered to: 1am Thursday Kenyan time on a South African account, 3am on a UK one. Kenya is not on either console store's country list, so players buy through foreign accounts, at about KSh10,400 to KSh12,000 for the standard edition. The download can be up to 212 GB.
