Kenya slips to third in Africa's startup funding table

Kenya raised $126 million in H1 2026 — its weakest half since early 2021 — falling behind Egypt ($327M) and Nigeria ($254M) after leading the continent with $984 million in 2025. Africa: The Big Deal data points to investor caution after shutdowns including Koko Networks, Lipa Later, and Ilara Health, with VCs pressing founders on paths to profitability.

Source: Business Daily Africa

FINTECH

Safaricom cuts M-Pesa fees for businesses in sweeping tariff revision

From 7 August: Pochi transactions to KSh 200 free, Buy Goods free threshold doubled to KSh 500 (0.55% above, capped at KSh 200), and till transfers down up to 50% — aimed at MSMEs and aligned with CBK pricing principles.

POLICY

Kenya's national AI policy heads toward Cabinet after public consultation

The draft policy — a National AI Steering Committee, a Kenya AI Safety Institute, a public registry, and AI in Kenyan languages — closed comments on 4 August and now moves toward Cabinet approval alongside the AI Bill in Parliament.

FINTECH

Ziidi Trader puts the NSE inside 35 million M-Pesa wallets

Safaricom's in-app stock trading, built with Kestrel Capital, lets users buy NSE shares without a brokerage account — quickly hitting ~40% of trades. The NSE targets 9 million retail investors by end of 2029.

INFRASTRUCTURE

M-Pesa's "Fintech 2.0" rebuild targets a $14.5B payments market

Cloud-native with AI fraud detection, capacity up from 4,500 to 6,000 transactions per second (scalable to 12,000). M-Pesa earned KSh 161.1B in FY2025 — 44% of Safaricom's service revenue — holding ~90.8% of Kenya's digital payments.

Ecofin Agency

GOVERNMENT

ICT Authority tightens risk management on national digital programmes

The board and management met 20 August to review delivery risks across public Wi-Fi, institutional connectivity, and digital skills programmes — with a focus on resilience, accountability, and faster delivery.
