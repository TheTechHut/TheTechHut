Google will withhold 5% of Kenyan creators' YouTube earnings for KRA

From September earnings — paid out in October — Google deducts a 5% withholding tax on Kenya-based AdSense for YouTube accounts, implementing the digital content monetisation tax introduced in the Finance Act 2023 (Treasury first proposed 15% before public pressure cut it to 5%). Creators must have a verified KRA PIN on file by 1 October or payments are held until they do; earnings keep accruing in the meantime. The 5% is an advance tax, and sits on top of the US withholding of up to 30% that Kenyan creators already face on US-sourced views. A creator earning KSh 100,000 a month sees KSh 5,000 go to KRA before anything else.

NCBA and BasiGo strike deal to finance 1,000 electric matatus

NCBA becomes BasiGo's first local financing partner, offering PSV SACCOs up to 90% asset finance over 60 months (80% over 48 for individual members) at a discounted 1.5% processing fee, alongside BasiGo's Pay-As-You-Drive lease. The deal backs BasiGo's target of 1,000 electric buses on Kenyan roads by 2027 — 53 assembled locally so far, 27 in production — helped by zero-rated VAT on e-buses under February's National Electric Mobility Policy.

SUN Mobility launches an open battery-swap network from Nairobi

India's SUN Mobility switched on an open-architecture battery-swapping network in Nairobi and Mombasa — 35 stations live, with 10+ vehicle makers including Piaggio and VMoto on the platform. A Vivo Energy partnership opens the door to 4,200+ Shell and Engen forecourts across 29 African countries, with a five-year target of 2,500 stations and 160,000 vehicles, and claimed running-cost savings of 20-35% over petrol.

HFCB posts record half-year profit as its capital raise pays off

Three months after rebranding from HF Group, HFCB reported pre-tax profit of KSh 1.23 billion for H1 2026 — up 74.3% and past the KSh 1 billion half-year mark for the first time — with deposits up 29.7% to KSh 68 billion. The December 2024 rights issue that raised KSh 6.38 billion funded the technology upgrades and expansion now feeding the rebound.

4G Capital crosses $1 billion in loans to Kenya's market traders

The Nairobi-based lender founded by ex-British Army officer Wayne Hennessy-Barrett has now disbursed over $1 billion across 7 million-plus unsecured working-capital loans, averaging $120-130 each, with a roughly 95% repayment rate. Its Eva credit algorithm scores repayment behaviour instead of collateral — reaching some 800,000 of Kenya's 7.2 million MSMEs in a region with a $19.3 billion small-business financing gap.

The 8GB/256GB Galaxy S26 FE goes on sale from 4 September with a 6.7-inch 120Hz AMOLED, 4,900mAh battery with 45W charging and a 50MP triple camera, promising seven years of OS and security updates.
