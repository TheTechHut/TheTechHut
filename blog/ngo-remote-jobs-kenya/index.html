NGO Remote Jobs in Kenya: Where to Find Them and How to Apply

Most NGO jobs in Kenya are tied to an office or a field site. The remote ones cluster in a few skills, at organisations already present here.

The Tech Hut · Wednesday, 25 November 2026

NGO remote jobs in Kenya exist, but fewer than the search results suggest. Most NGO roles based in Kenya are tied to an office or a field location, and many international NGO roles marked “remote” are open only in certain countries. The ones you can take from Nairobi tend to be in technology, data, communications, finance and grants, at organisations that already have Kenyan staff or legal presence. Knowing that narrows the search to the places worth checking.

This guide covers where NGO and development jobs are advertised, what “home-based” means in UN and NGO adverts, which skills are most in demand, and how to apply.

What NGO hiring in Kenya looks like right now

On 15 November 2026 our scan of employers' own hiring systems found 35 roles in Kenya at non-profit and social-impact organisations, including One Acre Fund (15 roles), Kasha, Tiko, the Wikimedia Foundation, OpenFn, Greenpeace International, TechnoServe, SNV, Teach For All and Ipas. Titles included agricultural trials lead, carbon finance manager, automation engineering lead and graphic designer.

Most of these were based in Nairobi or a named Kenyan location rather than fully remote. That is typical: NGOs value staff who can visit programmes and partners. Fully remote roles are more common in technology, data, communications and fundraising.

What “home-based” means in NGO and UN adverts

UN agencies and large NGOs often use “home-based” for consultancies and some staff roles. It means you work from where you live, but the advert may still limit who can apply: by nationality, by region, or by time zone. Read the eligibility section, and check whether the role is a staff contract or a consultancy, since consultancies are paid by deliverable or by day and usually carry no benefits.

Where NGO jobs are advertised

  • ReliefWeb: the humanitarian information service run by the UN lists jobs and consultancies from NGOs and UN agencies, filterable by country and type.
  • UN job portals: each agency has its own careers site, and aggregators such as UNjobnet and UN Talent collect them, including home-based consultancies open to Kenyan nationals.
  • Impactpool and Devex: international development job boards that list NGO, UN and donor roles.
  • NGOs' own careers pages: organisations such as One Acre Fund, GiveDirectly and the Wikimedia Foundation post on their own hiring systems. Our free jobs board reads several of these directly.
  • Our Remote-From-Kenya list: remote roles at organisations, NGOs included, that will employ you from Kenya.

Skills NGOs hire for remotely

  • Data and monitoring: monitoring and evaluation, data analysis, survey tools, dashboards.
  • Technology: software engineering, data engineering, digital product, IT support.
  • Communications: writing, editing, design, social media, video.
  • Finance and grants: grant management, donor reporting, finance operations.

NGOs hire for mission fit and for proof. Show both in the first half page of your CV.

How to apply to an NGO

NGO applications often ask for a cover letter that answers the selection criteria point by point, and sometimes for referees up front. Mirror the criteria in the order the advert lists them, give one concrete example for each, and keep to any word or page limit. Our guide on how to write a CV covers the CV side.

Be wary of anything that asks you to pay an application, processing or medical fee to an NGO or UN body. Genuine organisations do not charge applicants.

Where to start

Pick three organisations whose work you care about and check their careers pages weekly, set alerts on one NGO job board, and turn on our free daily job alert for new Kenyan roles each morning.
