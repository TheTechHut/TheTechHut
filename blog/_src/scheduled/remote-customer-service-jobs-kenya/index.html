Remote Customer Service Jobs in Kenya: Where to Find Them

Remote support roles at global companies lean technical; Kenyan ones lean operational. Here is where each kind is listed and what wins them.

The Tech Hut · {{DATE_LONG}}

Remote customer service jobs in Kenya come from three places: global software companies hiring support and customer success staff worldwide, outsourcing firms that run support for international brands, and Kenyan companies with support teams that work from home or hybrid. They suit people with clear written English, patience and steady internet. The best-paid ones ask for some technical knowledge, because “support” at a software company often means helping customers fix a product.

This guide covers where these roles come from, what they pay attention to when hiring, the set-up you need, and how to tell a real support job from a scam.

The three kinds of remote customer service work

KindTypical titlesWhat it asks for
Software company supportSupport engineer, customer success manager, technical supportProduct knowledge, troubleshooting, often some technical background
Outsourced support (BPO)Customer service agent, chat or email agent, content moderatorStrong English, typing speed, shift work, set targets
Kenyan employersCustomer operations officer, customer success, call centre agentLocal product knowledge, often Swahili, sometimes office days

What is open right now

On 15 November 2026 our scan of employers' own hiring systems found 14 customer support and success roles that a person living in Kenya could do remotely, at companies including Canonical, Supabase and PostHog. Titles ranged from Associate Linux Support Engineer and Cloud Support Engineer to Graduate Customer Success Manager. A further 13 customer-facing roles were based in Kenya, at employers such as Ajua, M-KOPA and Sun King, including a customer support internship.

The pattern is clear: remote support roles at global companies lean technical, while Kenyan roles lean towards operations and local customers. If you want the remote kind, a little technical knowledge goes a long way.

Where to look

  • Our Remote-From-Kenya list: remote roles, support included, at companies that will employ you from Kenya, updated every morning.
  • Our free jobs board: Kenyan employers' roles read from their own hiring systems, including customer-facing ones.
  • Outsourcing firms’ careers pages: large BPO companies with Kenyan operations advertise agent roles on their own sites. Check the advert for whether the role is remote or office-based.
  • Software companies you use: if you know a product well, check its careers page for support roles. Product knowledge is a real advantage in an interview.

What gets you hired

  • Written English: most remote support is chat and email. Your application itself is the first writing test.
  • Troubleshooting: show one time you diagnosed and fixed a problem step by step, in IT, a shop or a school.
  • Tools: name help-desk and chat tools you have used, such as Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom, and basic Google Workspace or Microsoft 365.
  • Calm under pressure: give an example of an angry customer you turned around.

Put these at the top of your CV. Our guide on how to write a CV shows how to turn duties into results.

In support, the cover letter is a writing sample. Make it short, clear and kind.

The set-up you need

Expect to need a laptop, a headset with a microphone, a quiet space and an internet connection with a mobile-data backup. Many roles ask you to pass a typing or internet speed test. Ask about shift times before you apply: supporting customers in the US or Europe can mean evening or night shifts in Kenya.

Red flags

Real support employers do not charge you for training, equipment or registration, and they interview you on video or phone. Walk away from any “customer service” offer that arrives only on WhatsApp, asks for a fee, or involves receiving and forwarding money.

Where to start

Decide which kind you are aiming at: technical support at a software company, agent work at an outsourcer, or a Kenyan employer. Build your CV around that kind, then check one source daily. Our free daily job alert emails new roles every morning.
