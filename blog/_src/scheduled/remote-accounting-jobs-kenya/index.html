Remote Accounting Jobs in Kenya: Who Hires and How to Get In

Remote finance roles open to Kenya cluster at a few international employers. Here is who they are, and what wins the interview.

The Tech Hut · {{DATE_LONG}}

Remote accounting jobs in Kenya are real but specific. The ones open to people in Kenya are mostly at international companies hiring finance staff for a Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) team, at outsourcing and bookkeeping firms serving foreign clients, and in freelance bookkeeping. Kenyan employers hire accountants too, but mostly on site or hybrid. What wins remote finance roles is a recognised qualification, fluency in a cloud accounting tool, and clean written English.

This guide covers who hires remote accountants from Kenya, the qualifications and tools they ask for, where to look, and what to settle before you sign.

What is open right now

On 15 November 2026 our scan of employers' own hiring systems found 17 finance and accounting roles that a person living in Kenya could do remotely, 14 of them at one open-source software company, Canonical. Titles included accountant, accounts payable clerk, accounts receivable clerk, revenue accountant, tax associate and payroll manager, mostly listed as home based in EMEA. A further 8 finance roles were based in Kenya, at employers including M-KOPA, One Acre Fund, Moniepoint, Apollo Agriculture and Standard Bank Group.

That split is typical. Remote finance roles cluster at a few international employers, so it pays to track those employers directly rather than wait for a job board to show them.

Freelance bookkeeperBookkeeping for small foreign clientsA cloud tool such as QuickBooks Online or Xero, references

Qualifications and tools that travel

Employers abroad need to trust a qualification they recognise. ACCA is widely known internationally; CPA (Kenya) is the standard locally and is recognised by many regional and international employers. Whichever you hold, write its full name on your CV.

Tools matter as much. Remote finance teams run on cloud systems, so list the ones you have actually used: QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage, NetSuite, SAP or Oracle, plus advanced Excel or Google Sheets. If you have only used one, learn a second well enough to show a sample reconciliation.

For a remote finance role, a named tool and a closed month you can describe beat a long list of duties.

Where to look

  • Our Remote-From-Kenya list: remote roles, finance included, at companies that will employ you from Kenya, checked every morning.
  • Employers' careers pages: companies with EMEA finance teams post roles on their own hiring systems first.
  • Employer-of-record and payroll companies: firms that employ people across many countries also hire finance staff remotely.
  • Freelance platforms: for bookkeeping clients, build a profile around one tool and one industry.
  • Our free jobs board: Kenyan finance roles from employers' own hiring systems.

What to settle before you sign

Ask whether you are a contractor or an employee, what currency you are paid in and by which platform, the hours of overlap with the team, and who provides the software licences. Month-end close often means busier days at fixed times, so ask about those too. Income from abroad can carry Kenyan tax obligations; as a finance professional you will know to confirm yours before the first payment.

Where to start

Update your CV so the first half page names your qualification, your tools and one close or reconciliation you owned, with a number. Our guide on how to write a CV shows how. Then track five employers with EMEA finance teams and check our list each week.
