CV Writing Format: 3 Samples for Kenyan Job Seekers

One safe format, three ways to fill it. Pick the sample that matches your situation and copy its structure.

The Tech Hut · Friday, 20 November 2026

The right CV writing format depends on your situation, but the safe default for most Kenyan job applications is the same: a one-column, reverse-chronological CV of one or two pages, with your name and contacts at the top, a three-line profile, then experience, education and skills. Below are three short samples built on that format, one for a graduate, one for someone with a few years of experience and one for a career changer, so you can copy the structure that matches you.

For the full step-by-step method, including how to write bullets and get past screening software, read our main guide on how to write a CV for a job application.

The standard CV format at a glance

PartFormat rule
PageA4, one column, about 2.5 cm margins, one or two pages
FontArial, Calibri or Times New Roman, size 11 for text, 14 for headings
TopYour name as the title (not “Curriculum Vitae”), then phone, email, city and one link
OrderProfile, Experience, Education, Skills, then extras; graduates may put Education before Experience
Leave outPhoto, date of birth, ID number, marital status, unless the advert asks
FileFirstname-Lastname-CV, as .docx for portals or PDF for email, unless the advert says otherwise

Sample 1: Graduate with no full-time job yet

Lead with education and projects. Treat an attachment, a volunteering role or a final-year project exactly like a job: title, dates, bullets with results.

BRIAN KIPCHUMBA

Eldoret, Kenya | +254 7XX XXX XXX | brian.kipchumba@email.com | github.com/briankipchumba

PROFILE

Computer science graduate who built a hostel booking app used by 120 students during final year. Looking for a junior developer or IT support role where I can grow on a team.

EDUCATION

BSc Computer Science | Moi University | 2021 to 2025

PROJECTS AND ATTACHMENT

Hostel Booking App | Final-year project | 2025

  • Built a web app in Django and PostgreSQL that 120 students used to book rooms in one semester

ICT Attachment | County Government office | May to Jul 2024

  • Set up and maintained 25 office computers and resolved user tickets, cutting the backlog from 40 to under 10

SKILLS

Python, Django, SQL, HTML/CSS, Git, Windows and Linux support, networking basics

Sample 2: Three to five years of experience

Lead with experience, newest first. Give your latest role the most bullets, each with a result, and shrink older roles.

GRACE WANJIKU

Nairobi, Kenya | +254 7XX XXX XXX | grace.wanjiku@email.com | linkedin.com/in/gracewanjiku

PROFILE

Digital marketer with four years in fintech and retail. Grew organic sign-ups 60% in a year at a lending app. Looking for a growth marketing role at a product company.

EXPERIENCE

Growth Marketing Lead | Lending app, Nairobi | 2023 to present

  • Grew organic sign-ups 60% in 12 months through SEO and referral campaigns
  • Cut cost per install by a third by moving budget from display ads to creators
  • Manage a team of two and a monthly budget across four channels

Digital Marketing Officer | Retail chain, Nairobi | 2021 to 2023

  • Ran social campaigns for 30 stores and launched the first email newsletter

EDUCATION

BCom Marketing | Kenyatta University | 2016 to 2020

SKILLS

SEO, Google Analytics, Meta Ads, email marketing, A/B testing, copywriting

Sample 3: Career changer

Put a short skills-with-proof section first, so the reader sees what you can do in the new field before your old job titles. Keep the history, briefly.

DAVID MUTUA

Mombasa, Kenya | +254 7XX XXX XXX | david.mutua@email.com | davidmutua.dev

PROFILE

Former high-school maths teacher moving into data analysis. Built three public dashboards from Kenyan open data. Looking for a junior data analyst role.

DATA SKILLS AND PROJECTS

  • School results dashboard: cleaned five years of exam data in SQL and built a Power BI dashboard used by the school's heads of department
  • County budget tracker: analysed open budget data in Python and published charts on my site

EXPERIENCE

Mathematics Teacher | Secondary school, Mombasa | 2018 to 2025

  • Taught 200 students a year and tracked results in spreadsheets used for department planning

EDUCATION AND TRAINING

BEd Mathematics | Pwani University | 2014 to 2018
Data analysis course certificate | 2025

SKILLS

SQL, Excel, Power BI, Python (pandas), presenting to non-technical audiences

Choose the format that puts your strongest proof in the top third of page one.

Which format to pick

  • Some relevant work history: Sample 2, reverse chronological.
  • Graduate or student: Sample 1, education and projects first.
  • Switching fields or returning after a gap: Sample 3, skills with proof first.

All three samples are for fictional people; replace every detail with your own, and keep only claims you can explain in an interview.
