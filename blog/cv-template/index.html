Free CV Template: Copy, Fill In and Send

Plain beats pretty. Copy this one-column template, replace the brackets, and it will read cleanly for both a person and a screening system.

The Tech Hut · Sunday, 15 November 2026

A good CV template is plain: one column, standard headings, no tables, icons or photo, so both a recruiter and a screening system can read it. Below is a free, copy-ready template in that format. Paste it into Word or Google Docs, replace each bracket with your own details, delete any line that does not help you win the job, and save it under your own name.

If you want the reasoning behind each section and how to write strong bullets, read our main guide on how to write a CV for a job application.

The free CV template

Select everything in the box, copy it, and paste it into a blank document. Then set the font to Arial or Calibri, size 11, and make the headings bold.

[YOUR FULL NAME]

[Town], Kenya | [+254 7XX XXX XXX] | [firstname.lastname@email.com] | [LinkedIn, GitHub or portfolio link]

PROFILE

[Job title or level] with [time or type of experience] in [field]. [One result with a number]. Looking for a [target role] at a [type of employer].

EXPERIENCE

[Job title] | [Employer], [Town] | [Month Year] to [Month Year or present]

  • [Action verb] [what you did], [result with a number]
  • [Action verb] [what you did], [result with a number]
  • [Action verb] [what you did], [result with a number]

[Earlier job title] | [Employer], [Town] | [Month Year] to [Month Year]

  • [Your strongest result in this role]

PROJECTS

[Project name] | [Tools used] | [Link]

  • [What you built and what it achieved]

EDUCATION

[Qualification, as written on the certificate] | [Institution] | [Year] to [Year]

SKILLS

[Skill named in the advert], [skill], [tool], [tool], [language]

CERTIFICATIONS

[Certificate name] | [Issuer] | [Year]

REFEREES

Available on request

How to fill in each part

SectionFill it withDelete it if
ProfileYour level, one proof with a number, the role you wantNever; rewrite it per job instead
Experience3 to 5 result bullets for your latest role, fewer for older onesYou have no work history; use Projects instead
ProjectsThings you built, with a linkYour experience already proves the skills
EducationQualification, institution, yearsNever
SkillsTools and methods the advert names, that you can discussNever

Why a plain template beats a designed one

Many employers in Kenya receive applications through hiring systems such as Greenhouse, Lever or SmartRecruiters, which turn your CV into plain text before anyone reads it. Columns, text boxes, tables, icons and skill bars often come out scrambled or missing. A designed template from a free template site can look striking and still lose you the interview. A plain one reads cleanly every time.

The best CV template is the one a machine reads as easily as a person.

Make it fit each job

Keep one master copy of the filled-in template with everything you have done. For each application, save a copy, then rewrite the profile and reorder the bullets so the advert's top requirements appear first, in the advert's own words where they are true for you. That takes about 15 minutes and makes more difference than any design choice.
