How it works

Four steps, one day

You pay and message us on WhatsApp

Send your CV in whatever state it is in — Word, PDF, a Canva export, a photo of a printout. Plus the link to the role you want, or the job title and company if you have not found the posting yet.

We identify the system behind the apply button

Oracle, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Pinpoint, JazzHR — each one mangles a CV differently. We already track which employer runs which; that research is the reason this service exists and it is why a generic CV writer cannot do it.

We rebuild it and run the parse check

One column, parser-safe headings, dates in the format the system expects, and your bullets rewritten as X-Y-Z with the numbers you actually have. Then we strip it to plain text to see exactly what the parser will see, and fix what breaks.

You get three things back

The rewritten CV as both .docx and .pdf, the keyword set for that role with a note on where each term should appear, and the plain-text parse so you can see what changed and why. Within 24 hours on a working day.

✅ One round of changes is included. Read it, tell us what you would say differently, and we fix it. It is your career history — we are formatting and sharpening it, not taking it over.

Fair questions

Before you ask

How is this different from the free CV pack?

The pack teaches you the rules and gives you a skeleton. This is us applying them to your CV, for a named employer, with the keyword research done. If you have the time and patience, do it yourself — the pack is genuinely complete.

Will you do more than one role?

Each booking covers one CV targeted at one role, because the keyword work is role-specific. A second role on the same CV is KSh 500 if booked at the same time.

What if I have no work experience yet?

Then say so when you book. Projects, coursework and internships can be framed with real numbers, and we will tell you which of yours carry weight — but be warned that a thin CV stays thin, and formatting cannot fix that.

Do you keep my CV?

We keep it while we work on it and delete it after your revision round, unless you ask us to hold it for a future booking. We never pass it to an employer without you telling us to.

Is 24 hours realistic?

On a working day, yes — that is why there are only ten slots a week. If you book on a Friday night, you get it Monday, and we will say so when you book.
